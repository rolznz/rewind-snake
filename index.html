🐍 Rewind Snake

Rewind Snake is a classic snake game with a time-traveling twist.


🎮 How to Play

Use Arrow keys or WASD to steer your snake (or swipe on touchscreen). Eat food to grow longer and score points.


⏪ Back in Time

Made a costly mistake? After game over, rewind by paying a small fee (100 sats/step). Step back through your last moves and recover from the error — giving yourself a second chance to survive!

🍊 Enhanced Mode

Rare oranges grant bonus growth. Walls appear over time, making survival even more intense.


🔥 Wall Breaker

In Enhanced Mode, press X (desktop) or tap the Wall Breaker button (mobile) to activate — smashes through walls for 3 seconds. Costs 3 segments.
